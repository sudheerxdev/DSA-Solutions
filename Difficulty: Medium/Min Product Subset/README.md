<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/max-and-min-products3347/1">Min Product Subset</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Medium</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p class="whitespace-pre-wrap break-words"><span style="font-size: 18.6667px;">Given an integer array <strong>arr[]</strong>, find the minimum<strong> </strong>possible product that can be obtained by multiplying the elements of any non-empty subset of the array.</span></p>
<p class="whitespace-pre-wrap break-words"><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre class="whitespace-pre-wrap break-words"><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input:</strong> arr[] = [1, 2, 3]
<strong>Output:</strong> 1
<strong>Explanation:</strong> The possible subset products are 1, 2, 3, 2, 3, 6, and 6. The minimum product is 1, obtained by selecting the subset [1].</span></pre>
<pre class="whitespace-pre-wrap break-words"><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input:</strong> arr[] = [4, -2, 5]
<strong>Output:</strong> -40
<strong>Explanation:</strong> The minimum product is -40, obtained by selecting the subset [4, -2, 5].</span></pre>
